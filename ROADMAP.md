# Jarvis app roadmap

**Live (phase 1)**
- Log in with your WhatsApp number and a one-time code. No password.
- Home: your S-ID and your bot's status.
- Alerts and a profile screen.

**Next**
- My Bot and bot settings.
- Support tickets.
- A plugin store, once plugins are available.

Plans can change as real users tell us what they need.
